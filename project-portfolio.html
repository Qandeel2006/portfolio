02 / The Context

Why it exists

As a Data Science student, I work across different areas of technology, from analytics and programming to visualization and web development.

I wanted to create a personal space that could bring my work together while reflecting my interests and evolving skills.

03 / The Challenge

What needed solving

I didn't want a website that simply listed my education, skills, and projects.

I wanted something that felt personal, was easy to explore, and could grow alongside my learning journey.

04 / The Journey

How it came together

I used HTML to structure the website, CSS to develop its visual identity and responsive layouts, and JavaScript to introduce interactions.

Throughout development, I experimented with navigation, animations, themes, and the overall user experience. Git and GitHub also became part of the process, helping me manage changes and track development.

05 / The Reveal

A small corner of the internet, built by me.

The result is a personal portfolio where I can share my projects, document my progress, and make my work accessible.

06 / The Reflection

What I took from it

Building this website taught me that creating a good digital experience involves much more than writing code.

It requires thinking about design, usability, responsiveness, and the way visitors interact with information. And, like my learning journey, this website is still evolving.

07 / The ending

Every project leaves something behind.

A good digital experience takes much more than writing code.

Sometimes it's a working application. Sometimes it's a new skill, a better question, or a completely different way of looking at a problem. These projects are part of my ongoing journey through Data Science and technology.

More experiments, more questions, more things to build.
