Legal / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. This site is a personal portfolio. It collects as little as possible.

What I collect

Contact form. If you send a message, I receive your name, email address and message so I can reply. Nothing else is asked for.

Analytics. I use Google Analytics 4 to see anonymous usage, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type and whether a contact form was completed. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers. You can block them in your browser or use Google's opt-out add-on.

Spam protection. The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell humans from bots. Turnstile may process technical signals such as your IP address and browser details. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Local storage. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent anywhere.

How it is used

Messages are used only to reply to you. I do not sell or share your details, and analytics data is used only to improve the site.

Services involved

Google Analytics (Google LLC), Cloudflare Turnstile and Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.), FormSubmit (delivers form messages to my email), Google Fonts, and GitHub Pages for hosting. Each has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask me to see or delete a message you sent by emailing qandeelqudsia@gmail.com. I keep messages only as long as needed to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
