Project 01 / Data Analytics

Numbers tell stories. The challenge is knowing where to look.

Imagine having thousands of sales records in front of you. Products, revenue, orders, regions, and endless rows of numbers.

Somewhere in all that information are patterns that can help explain how a business is performing. But how do you make those patterns visible? That's where this project begins.

02 / The Context

Why it exists

A business generates data every time a customer makes a purchase. But raw data alone doesn't make performance easy to understand.

I wanted to explore how sales information could be transformed into a visual experience that makes important metrics easier to interpret.

03 / The Challenge

What needed solving

The goal was to turn a collection of sales records into an interactive dashboard that could help users explore:

  • Overall sales performance.
  • Revenue patterns and trends.
  • Product-level performance.
  • Comparisons across different categories.
  • Important business metrics in one place.

04 / The Journey

How it came together

I started by exploring the dataset and understanding the information available.

Using Power Query, I worked with the data to prepare it for analysis. I then used DAX to create measures and calculated metrics that could support the dashboard.

The visualization stage was about more than choosing charts. I wanted the layout to guide the viewer's attention, making the most important information easy to find without overwhelming them.

05 / The Reveal

From rows of data to a clearer picture.

The final result is an interactive Power BI dashboard that brings key sales metrics and visual analysis together.

06 / The Reflection

What I took from it

This project helped me understand that a dashboard isn't simply a collection of charts. It is a way of communicating information.

I learned more about data preparation, DAX measures, visual hierarchy, and how design decisions can affect the way someone interprets data.

Future exploration: more detailed trend analysis, improved business recommendations, and additional interactive views.
