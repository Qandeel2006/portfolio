Project 02 / Data Visualization

Behind every organization is a story about its people.

An organization isn't just made up of departments, numbers, and targets. It's made up of people.

HR data can reveal patterns about a workforce, but those patterns are not always easy to recognize when information is scattered across tables and spreadsheets. I wanted to explore how visualization could make that information easier to understand.

02 / The Context

Why it exists

This project explores workforce-related information through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

The idea was to bring different HR metrics into a unified visual space and make the information easier to explore.

03 / The Challenge

What needed solving

How can employee-related data be presented in a way that is clear, organized, and meaningful?

I focused on creating visual summaries that allow viewers to explore workforce metrics and recognize patterns in the available data.

04 / The Journey

How it came together

I began by exploring the dataset and identifying the information that could be communicated visually.

Then came the visualization process: selecting appropriate charts, organizing the dashboard, and thinking about how different elements should work together.

I experimented with layout, visual hierarchy, and interactive elements to make the dashboard feel like a connected analytical experience rather than a collection of unrelated charts.

06 / The Reflection

What I took from it

This project strengthened my understanding of data visualization and dashboard storytelling.

It also helped me appreciate how important it is to consider the audience when presenting analytical information.

Future exploration: deeper workforce analysis, additional filters, and more focused insights based on the dataset.

07 / The ending

Every project leaves something behind.

Good analysis starts by remembering who the audience is.

Sometimes it's a working application. Sometimes it's a new skill, a better question, or a completely different way of looking at a problem. These projects are part of my ongoing journey through Data Science and technology.
